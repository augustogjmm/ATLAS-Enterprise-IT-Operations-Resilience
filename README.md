# ATLAS — Enterprise IT Operations & Resilience

**Executive Technology Command Center** · Architecture · IT Operations · Business Continuity · Governance

> Independent demonstration project, designed to illustrate executive technology decisions through a synthetic enterprise scenario. **All services, configurations, costs, incidents and figures are fictional**; none represent an employer's infrastructure or actual service levels.

## Why ATLAS

ATLAS explores how an IT leadership team can translate infrastructure topology and operational signals into understandable decisions: dependencies, business-service impact, disaster recovery readiness, RTO/RPO objectives, and prioritization.

The intended audience is Head of Infrastructure, IT Operations Manager, Technology Director, Enterprise Architect and technology-risk stakeholders.

## Current status

An initial interactive MVP is being prepared in a review branch. No deployment or production readiness is claimed.

## Scope and boundaries

- Simulated enterprise service map (not real telemetry).
- Deterministic disruption and dependency impact simulation.
- Explainable recovery objectives and trade-offs.
- Architecture decision records, automated verification and accessibility-conscious UI.
- No financial trading, no employer data and no external API credentials.
